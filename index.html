Welcome to Clear Wise

Clear Wise is an expense management application designed to help users organize shared expenses, manage rooms, track payments, calculate settlements and understand their spending.

This page explains our privacy practices and the terms that apply when using the Clear Wise application.

Offline First Core expense management is designed for local device storage.
No Mandatory Login No account is required to use the core app features.
User Control Users manage their own rooms and expense records.
Data Management Backup, restore and export options are available.

1. Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Clear Wise is designed to provide expense management without requiring users to create an online account.

The app's core functionality is designed to work offline. Information entered into the app is primarily stored locally on your device.

Clear Wise does not require you to submit your personal expense records to a Clear Wise account or cloud server to use its core functionality.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on how you use Clear Wise, the following information may be entered and stored in the application:

Room Information

  • Room names and descriptions.
  • Member names and room-related information.
  • Room-specific currency preferences.

Expense Information

  • Expense amounts and descriptions.
  • Payment and settlement records.
  • Budgets and spending information.
  • Activity history and edit history.

App Preferences

  • Selected currency and application settings.
  • User-configured preferences.

This information is provided by you. Clear Wise does not need access to your bank account or banking password to perform its normal expense calculations.

Clear Wise does not automatically create rooms, members or expenses. Users create and manage their own records.

3. Data Storage

Core application information is designed to be stored in the application's local storage/database on your Android device.

  • No mandatory online account is required.
  • Core expense features are designed to work offline.
  • Your records remain associated with your device.
  • Uninstalling the application may delete locally stored data.

We do not claim that local data is automatically backed up to an online server. You should maintain your own backup if you want to preserve your records.

4. Backup, Restore & Export

Clear Wise provides data management features so users can maintain and transfer their records.

Database Backup & Restore

Users can create a database backup and restore supported backup files when required.

External Storage Providers

Users may choose a storage destination through Android's file selection system, including a supported cloud storage provider such as Google Drive.

If you choose an external provider, your backup file may be transferred to that provider. Its privacy policy and security practices will apply to that service.

CSV Import & Export

Users can import or export supported room expense records through CSV files.

Backup and export operations are user-initiated. Review your backup files carefully and keep them secure.

5. Data Security

We aim to keep the application simple and privacy-conscious. Users should protect their Android devices with appropriate screen locks and security settings.

  • Do not share backup files with untrusted people.
  • Keep your device software updated.
  • Store exported files in secure locations.
  • Do not share sensitive expense information unnecessarily.

No digital storage method can guarantee absolute security. We cannot guarantee protection against every possible device failure, malware infection or unauthorized access.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Clear Wise includes a Trash Bin feature designed to automatically delete eligible trashed records after 30 days.

Users should review their Trash Bin before permanent deletion. Once records are permanently deleted, recovery may not be possible unless a valid backup exists.

Users can also manage their application data through available in-app controls and Android application settings.

Deleting information inside Clear Wise does not automatically delete copies previously exported or uploaded to external storage providers.

7. Third-Party Services

Clear Wise may interact with external services when users voluntarily use features such as file selection or external backup storage.

Such external services operate independently and are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

Clear Wise does not require a third-party account for its core expense management functionality.

Any future introduction of advertising, analytics, crash reporting or additional third-party services will require this policy to be reviewed and updated before release.

8. Children's Privacy

Clear Wise is a general expense management utility. It is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly request children's personal information through an online account registration system because the core application does not require such registration.
